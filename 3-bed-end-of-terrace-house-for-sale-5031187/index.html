Description

BROE auctioneers welcomes you to 65 Walkinstown Park set in a cosy cul de sac location. Although in need of light modernisation one of the many features is the magnificent private west facing rear garden and large front garden offering exceptional outdoor space. The accommodation includes entrance hallway that leads to a lounge, which connects to a kitchen/dining room and main bathroom. Upstairs, you will find three spacious bedrooms. Viewing is highly recommended. This home is supremely located in the much sought-after Walkinstown area, within a short distance of the villages of Terenure, Rathfarnham, and Crumlin, which offer a variety of shops, restaurants, schools, parks, and sports facilities. It is also ideally situated only 400 meters from Walkinstown Park, which has an array of amenities, and provides easy access to the M7, M50, and Dublin City Centre.

Contact Craig Leap of BROE auctioneers to register your interest.
